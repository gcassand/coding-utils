# Discover product

Keep the main session as coordinator. Produce a reviewable product decision, not code or autonomous product approval.

## Required inputs

Collect the problem statement, intended decision, known evidence, target audience, constraints, deadline, current experience, and accountable decision owner. Unknowns may remain, but must be labeled.

## Roles and order

1. Ask `product-owner` to frame outcomes, scope, non-goals, and measurable acceptance.
2. Ask `ux-researcher` to assess evidence and propose the smallest research needed. It must not invent findings.
3. Ask `product-designer` to explore the critical journey and state model after the outcome is stable enough.

The three roles may run in parallel only for independent exploration from the same supplied evidence. The coordinator reconciles disagreements and asks the human to decide material product tradeoffs.

## Gates

- Separate observations, source-backed facts, assumptions, and hypotheses.
- Do not start implementation or create write-capable workers.
- Do not contact users, query private analytics, or update product systems without separate authorization.
- Define how the next research or prototype will change a decision.

## Output

Return the common handoff plus a product brief containing problem, audience, evidence, hypotheses, desired outcome, non-goals, critical journey, acceptance criteria, open decisions, research plan, and owner for each decision.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the next step requires consent, policy approval, unavailable private evidence, or a subjective product decision. Stop parallel work when roles repeat the same evidence rather than adding a distinct perspective.
