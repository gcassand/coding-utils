The requested 31 August 2026 cutoff was still in the future.

## Interpretation

Evidence quality is claim-relative. **High** denotes a rigorous, directly relevant primary study or authoritative documentation for a capability claim; **medium** denotes useful primary evidence with material validity or reproducibility limits; **low** denotes anecdotal, promotional, indirect, or insufficiently reproducible evidence. **Mixed / claim-relative** commonly means high-quality evidence that a platform feature exists but low-quality evidence that the feature improves outcomes. Detailed findings and flags remain in the linked scope memos.
